Everyone using the same Campaign ID sees the same characters live. Make it something only your table knows, e.g. black-tortuga-42, rather than something guessable — see the setup guide for why.

🛌 Rest

Rests the whole party at once (Section 6). Short Rest: 1/10 of max HP and Mana back. Long Rest: half of what's missing back. A character who already rested in the last minute (here or on their own sheet) is skipped, so nobody heals twice. Each card below also has its own rest buttons.

🏝️ New Island

Fully restores HP and Mana and repairs broken potion bottles for every character in this campaign — the Island Reset moment (Section 11.3). Point catch-up and the shared bonus batch from that section aren't automated yet; handle those manually.
